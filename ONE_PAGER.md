# Artha: AI Munshi For Small Merchants

## One-Line Pitch

Artha is a mobile-first AI assistant for small merchants that remembers shop context, answers daily business questions, supports voice, and helps verify payments.

## Problem

Small merchants run the business through memory, notebooks, WhatsApp, payment screenshots, and scattered apps. They need fast answers, not another complex dashboard.

Typical questions are simple but operationally important:

- How much business happened today?
- Who still owes money?
- Which customers have stopped coming?
- Is this payment screenshot real?
- What should I follow up on tomorrow?

## Solution

Artha acts like a digital munshi:

- remembers shop context and customer notes
- answers in simple merchant language
- supports text, voice notes, and realtime voice
- grounds answers in business data and memory
- handles payment/OCR verification flows
- returns summaries, alerts, and lists in a mobile-friendly interface

## Why Now

Realtime voice, better speech-to-text, cheaper language models, and lightweight RAG make it possible to build a practical assistant for merchants without forcing them into traditional software workflows.

## Demo Flow

1. Create merchant account.
2. Add shop context during onboarding.
3. Ask: "Aaj kitna business hua?"
4. Ask: "Udhaar list dikhao."
5. Upload a payment screenshot.
6. Use realtime voice for a follow-up question.
